Partners and outreach

Working draft. Nothing on this page has been sent. All emails below are drafts for founder approval. Contact routes were checked on 2026-09-24 where marked "verified"; others still need checking.

The short answer

  • Talk to Windfall Trust first. It is building almost the same thing: a global endowment on behalf of all humanity, starting where the floor is lowest. Working with them could save years.
  • Ask for advice, not money, at first. E4H has no legal entity, so most funders cannot give to it yet. Early asks are: 20-minute feedback calls, introductions, and "what would you do differently?"
  • Keep asks small and specific. One clear question per email, with a link to the one-page summary.

The three named partners

OpenAI Foundation

  • What it is: the nonprofit that controls OpenAI Group PBC. It plans to invest at least $1 billion over the next year across life sciences, jobs and economic impact, AI resilience, and community programs, as part of a $25 billion commitment (OpenAI Foundation update; Resilience in the age of AI).
  • Current programs: AI Resilience focuses on bio-resilience, cyber-resilience, model safety, and AI's impact on young people. A Jobs and Economic Futures program was announced in 2026. The 2026 People-First AI Fund ($50 million) was only for eligible US 501(c)(3) community organizations and closed on 15 July 2026 (People-First AI Fund 2026).
  • Why a fit: E4H is exactly about sharing AI-era prosperity. The Jobs and Economic Futures program is the natural match.
  • What to ask for: nothing yet. Once E4H has a legal home, ask whether the Jobs and Economic Futures program funds research or pilots on shared ownership. Until then, follow announcements and publish work they would find useful (for example, the paper fund and take-up lessons).
  • Contact route: no open call found for E4H's type of project. Watch openaifoundation.org news (verified site).

World (World Foundation and Tools for Humanity)

  • What it is: World ID is a "proof of human" system. People verify once at an Orb (an eye-scanning device) and can then prove they are a unique human online without revealing who they are. World says nearly 18 million people are Orb-verified across 160 countries, and over 39 million have World IDs, about half verified (World ID announcement; World ID App launch). World App, which hosts "mini apps", is available in 150+ countries (World FAQ).
  • Why a fit: E4H needs a way to stop one person from claiming many times. World ID was designed for "one human, one claim". World App also already sends its own periodic "grants" to verified users, so World knows how to distribute to verified humans (Developer docs, user grant cycle).
  • Cautions: Orb access is limited in many places. Data-protection regulators in several countries have suspended or restricted World's operations [UNVERIFIED list: reported to include Kenya, Spain, Portugal, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Brazil, the Philippines]. Relying on one private company for identity is a dependency risk. E4H should support more than one verification method.
  • What building a mini app takes (from World's developer documentation):
    1. Register the app in the World Developer Portal.
    2. Build a mobile-first web app with the MiniKit software kit.
    3. Use the "verify" command with an action that allows one proof per human (so each person can claim once).
    4. Pass World's app review. The rules require a final version, "not a demo, trial or beta", no use of the word "World" in the name, and the app is responsible for being legal in every country where it is shown.
    5. Payments inside mini apps use crypto tokens on World's network; World covers transaction fees for verified users.
  • What this means now: E4H's prototype uses pretend data, so it cannot be listed yet. Agents can build a private test version using World's test tools to learn the flow.
  • What to ask for: later, a conversation about using World ID as one verification option for a pilot, and whether World's grants program covers public-good identity uses (its listed focus today is mostly blockchain infrastructure (World grants page)).
  • Contact route: Developer Portal at developer.worldcoin.org (verified in docs); grants at world.org/community-grants [not opened].

Moonshots podcast

  • What it is: a weekly technology and abundance podcast hosted by Peter Diamandis with co-hosts Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, and Alexander Wissner-Gross. It reports 1M+ listeners and 250+ episodes (moonshots.com). Its March 2026 episode with Andrew Yang covered basic income and AI job loss (episode #236).
  • Why a fit: the audience already believes AI will create great abundance. E4H answers the question "who will own it?" The project's own story mentions a Moonshots episode as an inspiration.
  • What to ask for: realistically, not a guest slot yet. First, publish the video series; then send a short pitch offering a 3-minute segment idea or a listener question: "If AI creates abundance, how does every human get a share?"
  • Contact route: press@moonshots.com (verified on site). No public guest form found.

How to reach out without overwhelm

  • Batch it. Agents prepare 3 to 5 drafts at a time. The founder approves and sends them in one sitting.
  • One ask per email, under 150 words, with a link to the plan summary.
  • Log everything in the status tracker: who, when, what was asked, reply.
  • Follow up once after about two weeks, then let it go.
  • Never imply E4H is a charity, accepts donations, or promises returns.

Draft email 1: aligned organization (for example Windfall Trust)

Subject: A small Canadian open-source project with the same goal as your global dividend fund

Hello [Name],

I lead Equity for Humanity (E4H), a volunteer, open-source project in Quebec. Our idea is close to your Global Dividend Fund: invest gifts in a broad world index and share the returns with verified people, starting where need is greatest. We hold no money and have no entity yet.

Rather than duplicate your work, I would like to learn how we could help. Would you have 20 minutes for a call in the coming weeks? Our public plan is here: equityforhumanity.org/plan/

Thank you, [Founder name], Equity for Humanity
