# Trump accounts: what they are and what E4H can learn

> **Working draft.** Facts checked on 2026-09-24. Rules for these accounts are still being finalized; proposed regulations were published in 2026 and could change.

## The short answer

- **Trump accounts** are new US government-backed investment accounts for children, created by the 2025 tax law (the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, section 530A of the US tax code). They launched on **4 July 2026**.
- **E4H cannot put money into them.** Only US governments and US 501(c)(3) charities can make "general" contributions, only through the US Treasury, and only in equal amounts to *every* child in a large group. E4H is Canadian and has no US entity.
- **E4H can learn a lot from them:** a seed deposit at birth, one low-cost index fund, a lock-up until adulthood, and a legal channel for philanthropists to top up many accounts at once. The early take-up data is also a warning about opt-in designs.
- **Best use for E4H:** learn from the design, track results, and use them in outreach and advocacy for a Canadian equivalent.

## Launch status (September 2026)

- Accounts went live on **4 July 2026**. Treasury reported over 7 million accounts opened, 1.7 million eligible for the $1,000 seed, and $1.5 billion invested by late July ([CNN, updated 23 Jul 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/04/business/trump-accounts-faq)).
- Some families reported waiting weeks for the $1,000; Treasury said most deposits arrive within one or two days ([Washington Times, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/22/trump-touts-trump-accounts-families-still-waiting-babys-1000/)).
- A September 2026 survey by the nonprofit Commonwealth found **only 5% of eligible low- and moderate-income families** had opened an account, even though 55% had heard of it ([Yahoo Finance, 22 Sep 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/trump-accounts-could-help-lower-income-families-most-few-have-signed-up-143843357.html)).

## The Dell pledge

- In December 2025, Michael and Susan Dell pledged **$6.25 billion** to put **$250** into the accounts of up to **25 million children** born 2016 to 2024 (age 10 and under) living in ZIP codes with a median family income of $150,000 or less ([CNBC, Dec 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/02/michael-susan-dell-trump-accounts.html); [Invest America eligibility](https://investamerica.org/dell/)).
- Deposits began on **31 August 2026**, starting in Texas and rolling out nationally. Only the first 25 million *activated* accounts receive it ([CNBC, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/trump-account-holders-to-start-receiving-250-dell-grant-spokesperson.html)).
- How the ZIP-code targeting fits the legal "qualified class" rules is **[UNCERTAIN]**; it may use designated geographic areas.

## What critics say

- **Opt-in leaves out the families who need it most.** Urban Institute points to research showing automatic enrolment is critical. Maine's Alfond Grant enrolled about 40% of newborns under opt-in and nearly 100% after switching to automatic enrolment in 2013 ([Urban Institute](https://www.urban.org/urban-wire/automatic-enrollment-not-opt-only-way-guarantee-trump-accounts-dont-leave-behind)).
- **Contributions favour families who can already save.** Wealthier families can add $5,000 a year; many low-income families cannot ([Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-are-trump-accounts-what-are-baby-bonds/); [Urban Institute](https://www.urban.org/urban-wire/without-clear-guidelines-trump-accounts-would-mostly-benefit-already-wealthy-families)).
- **Compared with "baby bonds"** (larger public deposits aimed at children with the least family wealth, as in Connecticut), Trump accounts are less targeted ([Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-are-trump-accounts-what-are-baby-bonds/)).

## What E4H could realistically do

| Idea | Realistic? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contribute to Trump accounts | **No, not now** | Needs a US 501(c)(3), must go through Treasury, must cover an entire qualified class in equal amounts. Even a small state class at $25 a child would cost far more than E4H will have for years. |
| Copy the design for all humans | **Yes, as design lessons** | Seed early, one low-cost index fund, low fee cap, lock-up, easy top-ups by philanthropists, automatic enrolment where possible. |
| Partner or share lessons | **Maybe** | Groups like Invest America (the Dell partner) and Commonwealth (the take-up researchers) study exactly the questions E4H faces. Worth one friendly email each. |
| Advocate a Canadian equivalent | **Yes, low-cost** | Canada already has pieces (below). E4H can write about them, and use Trump accounts as a hook in videos and posts. |
| Learn from the E4H difference | **Yes** | Trump accounts are US-only, US-stocks-only, children-only, and exclude ESG screens. E4H aims to be global, world-stocks, all ages, and lightly screened. That contrast is a clear story. |

## Lessons for E4H's design

1. **Opt-in alone will miss the people E4H most wants to reach.** Plan from the start for "connectors" (trusted local groups) who help people claim, and track take-up by income where possible.
2. **Unclaimed money is normal.** E4H's recycling rule (unclaimed benefits return to the fund) is sensible, but it should be paired with reminders so recycling is a choice, not an accident.
3. **One simple, cheap fund works.** Trump accounts use a 0.1% fee cap and a handful of index funds. E4H's one-fund approach is in good company.
4. **Philanthropic top-ups at scale are possible** when there is a legal rail. The Dell gift reached millions of accounts through one channel. A future E4H could offer a similar "top up everyone in a group" channel.
5. **Screens can be excluded by rule.** The US proposed rules keep ESG funds out of Trump accounts. If E4H ever connects with government-run accounts, its screen may not be allowed.
