# Partners and outreach

> **Working draft.** No correspondence has been sent. All messages below are drafts for founder approval. Contact routes marked "verified" were confirmed on the organization's website on 2026-09-24; others still require confirmation.

## In brief

- **The first aim is learning, not funding.** Equity for Humanity has no legal entity, so most funders cannot yet support it. Initial requests are for feedback, advice, and introductions.
- **Windfall Trust is a similar initiative** to learn from and possibly work with. It is developing a global dividend fund with a comparable purpose.
- **Requests are brief and specific,** with one question per message and a link to the published plan.

- **About:** the nonprofit that controls OpenAI Group PBC. It expects to invest at least $1 billion over the coming year across life sciences, jobs and economic impact, AI resilience, and community programs, as part of a $25 billion commitment ([OpenAI Foundation update](https://openai.com/index/update-on-the-openai-foundation/); [Resilience in the age of AI](https://openaifoundation.org/news/resilience-in-the-age-of-ai)).
- **Current programs:** the AI Resilience program focuses on biosecurity, cybersecurity, model safety, and the effects of AI on young people. A Jobs and Economic Futures program was announced in 2026. The 2026 People-First AI Fund ($50 million) was open only to eligible US 501(c)(3) community organizations and closed on 15 July 2026 ([People-First AI Fund 2026](https://openaifoundation.org/news/2026-people-first-ai-fund)).
- **Relevance:** Equity for Humanity addresses how the benefits of AI-driven growth could be broadly shared, which corresponds most closely to the Jobs and Economic Futures program.
- **Proposed request:** none at present. Once a legal entity exists, enquire whether that program supports research on shared ownership. In the meantime, follow its announcements.
- **Contact route:** no open call applicable to this project was found. Announcements are published at [openaifoundation.org](https://openaifoundation.org/) (verified).

### World (World Foundation and Tools for Humanity)

- **About:** World ID is a proof-of-personhood system. A person verifies once at an Orb, an iris-imaging device, and can then prove online that they are a unique person without disclosing their identity. World reports nearly 18 million Orb-verified people in 160 countries, and more than 39 million World IDs, about half of them verified ([World ID announcement](https://world.org/blog/announcements/the-new-world-id-and-the-partners-bringing-proof-of-human-to-the-internet); [World ID App](https://dev.world.org/blog/announcements/world-id-app-proof-of-human)). World App, which hosts third-party "mini apps", is available in more than 150 countries ([World FAQ](https://world.org/faqs)).
- **Relevance:** the Founding Concept Brief lists World ID among the verification methods to explore. It is designed to allow one claim per person.
- **Considerations:** Orb access is limited in many regions. Data-protection authorities in several countries have suspended or restricted World's operations **[UNVERIFIED list of countries]**. Reliance on a single private provider would create a dependency, so more than one verification method should be supported.
- **Requirements for a mini app,** according to World's developer documentation ([docs.world.org](https://docs.world.org/)):
  1. Register the application in the World Developer Portal.
  2. Build a mobile-first web application with the MiniKit software development kit.
  3. Use the "verify" command with an action that permits one proof per person.
  4. Pass World's review. Applications must be a final version, "not a demo, trial or beta", must not use the word "World" in their name, and are responsible for complying with the law in every country where they are shown.
  5. In-app payments use crypto tokens on World's network.
- **Implication:** the current prototype uses simulated data and cannot be submitted for listing. A private test version could be built to evaluate the verification flow.
- **Proposed request:** at a later stage, a discussion of World ID as one of several verification options for a pilot.
- **Contact route:** the World Developer Portal at [developer.worldcoin.org](https://developer.worldcoin.org/) (verified in documentation).

## Approach to outreach

- **Batches:** agents prepare several drafts at a time for the founder to review and send together.
- **Brevity:** one request per message, under about 150 words, with a link to the plan.
- **Records:** each contact, request, and reply is logged in the status tracker.
- **Follow-up:** one follow-up after about two weeks, then no further contact unless invited.
- **Accuracy:** no message may imply that Equity for Humanity is a charity, accepts contributions, or offers any return.
